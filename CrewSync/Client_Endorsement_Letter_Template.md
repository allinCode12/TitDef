### SUBJECT: Letter of Endorsement and Project Partnership for Capstone Study

Dear Esteemed Panel Members,

I am writing to formally confirm that **Kiddie Salon by Nail Brat** agrees to serve as the industry partner and beneficiary for the Information Technology Capstone Project proposed by students of FEU Diliman under the research team **AzTech**:

* **Proposed Project Title:**  
  *CrewSync: An AI-Assisted Web and Mobile-Based System for Event Staffing, Predictive Response Analytics, and Schedule Conflict Prevention for a Party Service Business*

**1. Nature of the Business Need:**  
Kiddie Salon by Nail Brat provides specialized kiddie pampering, nail art, and face painting services for birthday parties and events. Managing the scheduling and confirmation of our freelance/on-call talents across multiple concurrent events is currently done through informal messaging groups. This process has become time-consuming, prone to delayed responses, and increases the risk of scheduling overlaps during peak party seasons.

**2. Scope of Partnership & Support:**  
To support the academic objectives and system development of the team, our business agrees to provide:
* Necessary operational information regarding our service tiers, staffing requirements, and event workflows.
* Access to conduct interviews and surveys with the owner/management and participating talents.
* Participation in usability walkthroughs, prototype testing, and final system evaluation.

**3. Data Privacy and Confidentiality:**  
We acknowledge that all business and personal information gathered will be treated with utmost confidentiality in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Sensitive customer information will be anonymized or excluded from the academic documentation.

**4. Conflict of Interest Disclosure:**  
We openly acknowledge that a member of the student research team is a relative of the business owner. We confirm that this project was initiated based on a genuine operational requirement of our business, and our cooperation will provide authentic operational data and direct stakeholder engagement throughout the project lifecycle.

Thank you for supporting this collaboration between academia and local micro-enterprises.
