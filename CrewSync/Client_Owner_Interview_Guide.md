# Client Owner Quick Interview Guide (Fact-Finding)
*(Use this 15-minute interview with the owner of Kiddie Salon by Nail Brat to replace `[TO VALIDATE]` tags before your defense)*

---

### Section 1: Business Operations & Event Volume
1. **Average Monthly Events:** On average, how many party events do you handle per month? (During off-peak vs. peak months like December/summer?)
2. **Talent Roster Size:** How many active talents (face painters, nail artists, hair stylists) are currently on call?
3. **Talent Employment Status:** Are they full-time employees, or on-call freelancers who are free to accept/decline bookings?

---

### Section 2: Package Tiers & Staffing Rules
4. **Package Breakdown:** What are your standard party packages and what talents does each need?
   - *Example:* Package A (Basic Face Paint) = 1 Face Painter
   - *Example:* Package B (Full Kiddie Salon) = 2 Nail Artists + 1 Hair Stylist + 1 Face Painter
5. **Event Duration:** How long does a typical kiddie party service run? (e.g., 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours?)
6. **Travel Window:** How much buffer time is needed between back-to-back events to account for travel/traffic in Metro Manila? (e.g., 1.5 to 2 hours?)

---

### Section 3: Current Staffing Workflow & Pain Points
7. **Current Procedure:** Step-by-step, how do you assign a talent once a client confirms a booking?
   *(e.g., Post in a group chat? Message individually? Wait for replies?)*
8. **Staffing Time (Baseline Metric):** How long does it usually take from when an event is booked until all needed talents are 100% confirmed? (e.g., 6 hours? 24 hours? 2 days?)
9. **Past Incidents:**
   - Have you ever experienced a talent canceling last minute or not showing up? How did you solve it?
   - Has there ever been a near-miss or double-booking incident?
10. **Follow-up Friction:** How many follow-up messages do you typically send before getting a response from everyone?

---

### Section 4: Device & System Readiness
11. **Smartphones & Connectivity:** Do all your talents use smartphones (Android / iPhone) and have access to mobile data or Wi-Fi?
12. **Acceptance Window:** What would be a fair response deadline for talents to accept or decline an event invite before the system offers it to someone else? (e.g., 24 hours for events booked weeks ahead, or 2–4 hours for rush bookings?)
