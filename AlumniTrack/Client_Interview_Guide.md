# Client Office Interview Guide (Fact-Finding) — AlumniTrack
*(Use this 20-minute interview with the prospective client office — alumni/registry/careers/ICT — to clear Gate 0/Gate 2 and replace `[TO VALIDATE]` tags before your defense)*

---

### Gate 0: Before the interview — pick the right office

Approach offices that **actually hold graduate-outcome responsibility**. Screen with one message:

> *"We are BSIT capstone students (Team AzTech, FEU Diliman) developing a web and mobile system to help institutions monitor graduate employment status and course-to-career alignment. We are looking for the office that handles tracer studies/alumni surveys so we can learn your current process. We are not selling anything, and we will disclose how we use the data."*

| Candidate office | Why approach | If they say "that's not us" |
|---|---|---|
| Alumni / Placement & Careers Office | Most likely process owner | Ask who conducts tracer surveys |
| Registrar / Institutional Research | Holds graduation cohorts & reporting | Redirect per their answer |
| ICT / Development Office | System owner, data hosting | Ask which office owns alumni data |
| College Dean's / Program Chairs | Consumers of course-to-career data | Useful second interview |

**Gate 0 closes only when:** (a) the interview is completed, **and** (b) the office agrees in principle to endorse — letter signed before mock defense.

> **Own-institution note:** all candidates are offices within FEU Diliman, our own school. There is no relative involved (no COI clause needed), but two cautions apply: (1) the client must be the **office that owns the process**, formally represented by its head — not a teacher or friend doing the team a favor; (2) disclose in the defense that we are students of the institution we are approaching.

---

### Section 1: Organization & Role
1. **Your Role:** Which office is responsible for tracking where graduates end up — and is that you? Who else is involved?
2. **Reporting Obligations:** Why does the institution track graduate employment? (Accreditation? Internal reporting? Leadership requests?) Who consumes the figures, and how often?
3. **Authority:** If a system is built for your office, who approves it and who would use it daily?

---

### Section 2: Current Process (as-is workflow)
4. **Step-by-Step:** Walk us through the last tracer/survey campaign — from "we need the data" to "the report is done." (Tool? Who is contacted? How? Who consolidates?)
5. **Tooling:** What tools are used today — survey forms, spreadsheets, email, social media, existing software? *(Ask to see a blank form/template if possible — request de-identified copies.)*
6. **Cadence:** How often is this done? Is it one campaign per graduation batch, or continuous updates?
7. **Placement-Time Definition:** How does the institution define "job placement time"? (From graduation date? From recognition date? To first job? To first *related* job?) *(This decides our BR-A02.)*

---

### Section 3: Volume, Response & Pain Points *(the baseline metrics)*
8. **Graduate Volume:** How many graduates per year, across how many programs?
9. **Actual Response Rate:** What was the response rate of the last cycle? *(Ask for records/numbers — do not accept estimates as fact.)*
10. **Consolidation Effort:** How long does it take to produce one consolidated employment/alignment report, and who does it?
11. **Known Gaps:** What is hardest about the current process — reaching graduates, getting updates between campaigns, consolidating, producing program-level views? *(Listen for pain; do not feed them ours.)*
12. **Course-to-Career Alignment:** How is "course-to-career alignment" judged today, if at all? Who decides which jobs count as aligned per program?

---

### Section 4: Data, Privacy & Readiness (RA 10173)
13. **Data Ownership:** Who owns the graduate data today? Where is it stored, and who can access it?
14. **Consent & Retention:** Is there an existing consent/privacy notice for graduates? How long is data kept? Is there a Data Privacy Officer (DPO) we must coordinate with?
15. **Field Sensitivity:** Which fields may we collect? (Position, industry, start date, salary band?) Is salary acceptable, optional, or prohibited?
16. **Reach Channels:** How can graduates realistically be reached — email, FB page, SMS? What devices do graduates typically use? *(Feeds the web-vs-mobile decision.)*

---

### Section 5: Endorsement Close
17. **Interest:** Would a system that lets graduates self-report from their phones, with a dashboard for your office, be useful — or does your current process already cover this?
18. **Commitment:** If this fits your need, will your office:
    - let us interview/observe and review de-identified forms/reports,
    - allow us to survey a sample of graduates,
    - **sign an endorsement letter** for our capstone?
19. **Scope Guard:** If they ask for extras (job board, recruiter portal, AI matching): *"Noted — we will log that as a future-work request; our current version must stay small enough to deliver and evaluate honestly."*
