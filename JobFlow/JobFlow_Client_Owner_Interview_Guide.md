# Client Owner Quick Interview Guide (Fact-Finding) — JobFlow
*(Use this 15-minute interview with the owner/foreman of a print shop to replace `[TO VALIDATE]` tags before your defense. Ask, don't pitch. Run it on 2–3 shops to de-risk G1.)*

---

### Section 1: Business Operations & Job Volume
1. **Job Volume:** On average, how many print jobs do you handle per day? (Peak vs. off-peak — e.g., thesis season, holidays, election/tarpaulin rush?)
2. **Job Types:** What do you print most? (Tarpaulin/large-format, thesis/album, shirts/DTF, documents, stickers, signage?)
3. **Peak Triggers:** What makes the queue suddenly explode — a season, one big client, walk-ins?
4. **Staff:** Who runs the machines, and who decides the print order?

---

### Section 2: Production Setup (the machines)
5. **Machine List:** What machines do you have? (Digital press, offset, large-format printer, cutter, laminator, binder, heat press?)
6. **Job Routing:** Can one job run on more than one machine, or is each job locked to one?
7. **Prep Steps:** For a typical job, what are the steps, in order? (e.g., file prep → print → cut → laminate → finish?)
8. **Dependencies:** Does any step have to wait for another before it can start?

---

### Section 3: Current Scheduling Workflow & Pain Points
9. **Current Procedure:** Step-by-step, how do you decide *what to print next* once several jobs are lined up? *(Whiteboard? Excel? Memory? Group chat?)*
10. **Queue Visibility:** Where does the active queue live right now, and can everyone see it?
11. **Scheduling Time (Baseline Metric):** How long does it take to figure out the day's plan? (e.g., 15 min? An hour? All day?)
12. **Idle / Starvation:** Has a machine ever sat idle while other work was waiting? When and why?
13. **Rush Jobs:** How do you slot in a rush order? What breaks in the plan when one lands?
14. **Late Deliveries:** How often do jobs miss their promised pickup/deadline? What causes it?
15. **Follow-up Friction:** What do you or your staff *retype, re-check, or follow up on* repeatedly about the queue?

---

### Section 4: Switchover / Setup Details (the sequence-dependent setup angle)
16. **What Changes on Switch:** When you go from one job to the next, what has to change? (Paper stock, ink/color, size, laminate, temperature?)
17. **Setup Time:** Roughly how long does each switch take? Which switches are the worst?
18. **Batching:** Do you try to group similar jobs together to save time? How do you decide?

---

### Section 5: Device & System Readiness
19. **Smartphones & Connectivity:** Do you and your operators use smartphones with mobile data/Wi-Fi?
20. **Desired Mobile Use:** Would operators want to confirm job start/finish from the machine floor on their phones?
21. **Acceptance of Change:** What would need to be true for you to actually use a scheduling board instead of your current method?

---

### Section 6: Client Endorsement (G1 — the only gate that matters)
22. **Process Owner:** Who here owns this scheduling process, and can you be our client for our capstone?
23. **Access:** Can we observe operations and interview staff for a few weeks?
24. **Success:** If this system cut idle time and late pickups, would you endorse it and confirm the results?
25. **Disclosure:** *(Record honestly any relationship between the team and this shop.)*
