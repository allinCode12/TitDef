--- p40 ---
The story expresses a goal from a user perspective, while the criteria define 
observable completion.
A user story states value; acceptance criteria bound it
As a tourism reviewer, I want incomplete guide submissions flagged 
before review so that avoidable returns are reduced.
Acceptance direction
• Required fields are checked before submission.
• Missing fields are identified beside the relevant entry.
• The submission is not forwarded until errors are resolved.
